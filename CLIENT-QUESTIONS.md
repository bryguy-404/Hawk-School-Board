# Hawkins for Schools client questions

The first draft uses all wording from “2026_Sep_Hawkins for Schools webpage content.docx.” These questions are for launch review; photos and additional content are not needed to review this draft.

## Details needed before launch

- **Public name:** What exact name and spelling should appear publicly? Confirm “Hawkins for Schools” as the website name.
- **Role, district, and seat:** What is his exact current or intended role, the full district name, and the seat or position? Confirm any title he is authorized to use. The draft retains the supplied references to school board members and South Bend without inferring a district or seat.
- **Website purpose:** Is this a campaign website, a board-member information page, or another kind of personal information site? Confirm who owns and speaks through the website. It is not presented as an official school district website.
- **Content approval:** Are the three belief statements and eight priorities approved exactly as supplied, in their current order? Confirm any edits before launch.
- **Public contact details:** Which email address, phone number, or social links, if any, are explicitly approved for publication? It is fine to confirm that no contact information should be published. No form is included; a future form would need its own request and scope.
- **Domain and ownership:** What domain should be used, who owns it, and who will coordinate domain and Cloudflare account access? Do not send passwords by email; account invitations can be arranged separately. No domain has been assumed or connected.
- **Launch deadline:** What is the target launch date, and are there any timing constraints?
- **Final approver:** Who has authority to approve the wording, design, and publication, and how should final approval be recorded?
- **Footer wording:** Is any attribution, disclosure, or other footer language required? Please provide the exact client-approved wording, or confirm that none is required. No legal disclaimer has been invented.
- **Launch authorization:** Approve the final page and explicitly authorize publishing and search-engine indexing. The preview currently uses noindex metadata, a Cloudflare noindex response header, and a robots.txt crawl restriction.

## Optional improvements that can come later

- An approved headshot, with permission to use it and any required credit.
- A short, approved biography.
- Branding preferences: colors, fonts, examples, or an existing logo if one becomes available. The current wordmark and palette are provisional.
- Additional approved photography, only if it adds useful context.

These optional items are not blockers for this draft or prerequisites for a text-led launch.
